# Human Escalation

Escalate before proceeding when requirements, user impact, or acceptance criteria conflict or are incomplete; an allowed-path boundary must expand; a security, privacy, credential, destructive-data, licensing, accessibility, or production-provider risk appears; baseline verification fails; an external service, key, paid call, or manual test is required; a merge conflict occurs; or three review rounds fail to resolve the job. Record the question, evidence, alternatives, and affected files in the job and `docs/product/open-questions.md`.
